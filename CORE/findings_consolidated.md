Why the result covers all eight sectors
Adding 2mΓα preserves divergence and parity but changes Aα to Aα + 2mL. For sufficiently large positive m, the toggle probability is ½[1 + exp(−(L + 2Aα + 4mL)/(2J))], which varies strictly with m. Every nonempty parity fiber therefore contains a witness. All eight sectors admit valid states.
The independent checker also constructed 24 valid pairs: eight sectors × three axes at L = 8, J = 1. Every pair passed divergence/parity checks and had unequal toggle probabilities. The general proof supplies the domain-wide conclusion; those computations corroborate it.
