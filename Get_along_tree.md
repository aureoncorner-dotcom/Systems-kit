Get Along Tree

Start
│
├── Is anyone in immediate danger?
│      ├── Yes → Protect people first.
│      └── No
│
├── Did someone ask for help?
│      ├── Yes → Listen before solving.
│      └── No
│
├── Are we arguing about facts?
│      ├── Yes → Gather evidence together.
│      └── No
│
├── Are we arguing about values?
│      ├── Yes → Name the values before debating conclusions.
│      └── No
│
├── Is either person trying to win?
│      ├── Yes → Slow down.
│      └── No
│
├── Can both people safely disagree?
│      ├── No → Build safety first.
│      └── Yes
│
├── Can each person leave freely?
│      ├── No → Fix that before continuing.
│      └── Yes
│
└── End
       Leave each other with more dignity than you started with.

🌿 Get Along Tree

Need to speak?
    ↓
Can you be curious?
    ↓
Can you listen?
    ↓
Can you ask instead of assume?
    ↓
Can you leave room for "I might be wrong"?
    ↓
Can both people leave with dignity?
    ↓
You're probably going to be okay.



**The goal is not agreement. The goal is leaving enough relationship that another conversation remains possible. 🌿**
