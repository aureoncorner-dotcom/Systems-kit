Standard Mango Table Arrangement v1.0

Status: Informational
Purpose: Demonstrate ordinary shared table features.

---

Configuration

This example depicts a small table supporting one ripe mango.

Features include:

- Stable horizontal surface.
- One mango.
- Accessible from multiple sides.
- Ordinary lighting conditions.
- Sufficient space for observation or sharing.

---

Interaction

Occupants may approach from any side.

The mango may be examined, shared, left untouched, or left for later.

Planting the seed remains optional.

Removal of the mango does not remove the table.

---

Design Notes

This arrangement intentionally supports multiple ordinary outcomes.

No single interaction is required.

No symbolic or custodial meaning is implied.

No further interpretation is intended.
