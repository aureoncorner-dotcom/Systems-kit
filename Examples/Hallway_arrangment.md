Standard Hallway Arrangement v1.0

Status: Informational
Purpose: Demonstrate ordinary pedestrian circulation features.

---

Configuration

This example depicts a standard interior hallway.

Features include:

- Continuous walking surface.
- Doorways positioned along one or both sides.
- Overhead lighting.
- Unobstructed path of travel.
- Sufficient width for routine passage.
- Clearly identifiable direction of direction.

---

Circulation

Occupants may travel in either direction.

Occupants may pass one another without requiring either individual to leave the hallway.

Doorways may be entered or ignored at the occupant's discretion.

Stopping to converse does not prevent continued movement by others.

---

Design Notes

This arrangement intentionally supports independent movement.

Travel in one direction does not prohibit travel in the opposite direction.

The hallway serves access rather than destination.

No occupant is required to obtain permission from another occupant to continue walking.

No symbolic or custodial meaning is implied.

No further interpretation is intended.
