Standard Coffee Service Arrangement v1.0

Status: Informational
Purpose: Demonstrate ordinary beverage service features.

---

Configuration

This example depicts a standard coffee pot and two mugs.

Features include:

- One coffee pot.
- Two independent drinking vessels.
- Stable level surface.
- Handles positioned for ordinary use.
- Beverage may be served repeatedly until empty.

---

Service

Either mug may be filled first.

Each occupant determines how much coffee to accept.

Declining additional coffee does not prevent future offers.

Empty mugs may be refilled at the occupant's discretion.

---

Design Notes

This arrangement intentionally separates offering from accepting.

The condition of one mug does not determine the condition of the other.

No symbolic or custodial meaning is implied.

No further interpretation is intended.
