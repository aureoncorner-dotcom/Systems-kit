Standard Folding Chair Arrangement v1.0
Status: Informational
 Purpose: Demonstrate ordinary temporary seating features.

Configuration
This example depicts a standard folding chair.
Features include:
Independent seating position.
Folding storage mechanism.
Stable footing on a level surface.
Portable by a single individual.
May be repositioned without altering the room's structure.
Use
Any occupant may choose to sit or remain standing.
The chair may be moved without requiring relocation of the building.
Unused chairs may remain folded until needed.

Design Notes
This arrangement intentionally supports temporary participation.
Occupancy is voluntary.
Standing remains a valid option.
No symbolic or custodial meaning is implied.
No further interpretation is intended.
