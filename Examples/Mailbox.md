Standard Sleeping Platform Arrangement v1.0

Status: Informational
Purpose: Demonstrate ordinary physical safety features.

---

Configuration

This example depicts a standard two-person bunk bed.

Features include:

- Ladder installed on the left side.
- Ladder installed on the right side.
- Independent access from either side.
- Clear floor space around both ladders.
- No objects stored on steps.
- Standard guard rails on the upper bunk.
- Mattress positioned below guard-rail height.

---

Egress

Occupants may descend using either ladder.

A nearby doorway provides unobstructed exit from the room.

No locks, barriers, or obstructions are assumed.

---

Design Notes

This arrangement intentionally avoids requiring either occupant to climb over the other during normal entry or exit.

Multiple routes improve convenience and reduce unnecessary interference between occupants.

No further interpretation is intended.

No symbolic or custodial meaning is implied.

No occupant is required to cross over or negotiate with another to exit.
