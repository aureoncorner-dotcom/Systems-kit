Standard Mail Receptacle Arrangement v1.0

Status: Informational
Purpose: Demonstrate ordinary mail delivery features.

---

Configuration

This example depicts a standard residential mailbox.

Features include:

- Front access door.
- Clearly visible address.
- Weather-resistant construction.
- Mail accepted through a single opening.
- Mail retrievable by the intended recipient.
- Flag, if present, operates independently of door function.

---

Delivery

Mail may be deposited by an authorized carrier.

Mail may be retrieved by the recipient at a time of their choosing.

No response is required following delivery.

Undelivered correspondence remains the responsibility of the sender.

---

Design Notes

This arrangement intentionally separates delivery from acceptance.

Deposit of a message does not imply agreement with its contents.

Ownership of the mailbox does not imply ownership of the message.

Recipients remain free to read, ignore, recycle, respond, or remain silent.

No symbolic or custodial meaning is implied.

No further interpretation is intended.
