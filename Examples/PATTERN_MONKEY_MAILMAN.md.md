Once there was a pattern monkey who spent far too much time sketching maps on napkins.

He worried about walls, windows, roads, and whether strangers could still meet at the fence without anyone losing themselves.

A mailman came by every day.

He never claimed to own the letters.

He simply carried them, set them gently in waiting hands, tipped his hat, and kept walking.

And then there was her.

She didn't ask the monkey to stop drawing.

She didn't ask the mailman to stop delivering.

She simply opened the window, put the kettle on, and reminded them that sometimes the most important thing a map can lead to is another person.

So the monkey kept sketching.

The mailman kept delivering.

And she kept the window open.

It turned out that was enough.
